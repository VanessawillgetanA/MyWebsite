ABOUT

About

The short version, and the numbers behind it.

I'm a senior-year electrical engineering student at UCLA, finishing up a combined BS+MS with a focus on quantum science. My work sits at the point where physics turns into hardware: designing the microwave circuits that read out superconducting qubits, writing the digital logic that processes the result, and simulating both before anything gets built.

I came up through Ventura and Oxnard Colleges before transferring to UCLA, and I still think the habits I built there - showing up, asking the obvious question, keeping tidy notes - matter more than any one tool. I like problems that force me to work across layers, from RF fields in HFSS down to gate-level logic in Verilog.

Outside of coursework I keep a running list of side projects, mostly things I wanted to understand better myself: qubit readout simulators, an FPGA lock, a nuclear reactor kinetics model. Most of them are on GitHub, unpolished on purpose.

If you're looking at my GitHub and wondering about the username - it's a joke from freshman year that stuck. I did, in fact, get the A.
